一年，一艘船。

One year, one boat.

你是蘭嶼雅美／達悟族的工匠。照著海洋夜曆，從山上砍下 Avaka，把纖維處理成繩，填進拼板舟的縫裡。快一點的方法也有，只是每條路留下的東西不一樣。

You are a Tao (Yami) craftsperson on Lanyu. Following the night calendar, you cut Avaka on the hill, work its fibre into cord and caulk the seams of a plank boat. There are quicker ways too; each leaves something different behind.

Avaka · Musa textilis · 馬尼拉蕉麻Manila hemp

目標The aim

在第 12 個月結束前，造好自己的拼板舟。沒有誰贏誰輸：每個人依自己的分數，得到五種結局之一。

Finish your own plank boat before Month 12 ends. Nobody wins or loses: each player reaches one of five endings from their own score.

每個月大家各自用體力做事，全部按下「準備進入下個月」，就一起進到下個月。

Each month everyone spends their stamina in any order; when all press “Ready for next month”, the year moves on together.

開始Start

一個月裡會發生什麼，地圖怎麼走。

What happens in a month, and how to get around.

兩種點數Two kinds of points

體力 AP 是你的力氣與時間，幾乎每件事都要花。每個月開始時只回復一部分（文化復振青年 +6，耆老與中生代 +5），不會超過角色上限。技術 KP 是你對傳統工藝的了解，主要靠向人請益得到；後面的工序要有足夠的 KP 才能做。

Stamina (AP) is your strength and time; almost everything costs some. Each month you recover only part of it (Youth +6, Elder and Middle +5), never above your role’s limit. Skill (KP) is how well you understand the craft; you get it mostly by asking people, and the later steps need enough of it.

另外還有材料（處理好的 Avaka 纖維，整艘船要 5 份）和全組共用的森林狀態。

There is also material (prepared Avaka fibre; a boat takes 5) and a forest that the whole group shares.

一個月怎麼過How a month goes

  1. 新的月份開始。畫面上方說明這個月的效果，有些月份會先跳出事件卡要你選。
  2. A new month opens. The top of the screen shows this month’s effect; some months first hand you an event card.
  3. 各自行動。不用輪流，想做什麼就做，直到體力用完或不想再做。
  4. Everyone acts. No turns: do what you like until your stamina runs out or you are done.
  5. 按「準備進入下個月」。全組都按下後，一起進到下個月，大家回復一部分體力。
  6. Press “Ready for next month”. When everyone has, the group moves on and recovers part of its stamina.

工序Steps

一道一道來，跟著季節等。

One at a time, waiting on the seasons.

從砍下假莖到把纖維曬乾，這一整套 Avaka 的採集法，族語稱 ipana'ape so avaka（住田イサミ，1985）。

The whole Avaka method, from cutting the stem to drying the fibre, is called ipana'ape so avaka in Tao (Sumida, 1985).

  4. ④脫水曝曬乾燥Dry in the sun

    掛在竿子上曬乾。要曬多久，文獻記載 Avaka 大約五天；田野中看到的 Nyaga 做法是天氣好的夏天大約一天，冬天天氣差可能要兩天。潮濕會讓它腐爛，曬得太乾又會變脆、不好撚。

  • 依序做。做完①才能做②，依此類推。
  • In order. ① before ②, and so on.
  • 跨月等待。每做完一道，要到下個月才能做下一道。①可以重複；12 月的⑤和⑥可以同一個月接連完成。
  • Wait a month. After each step, the next waits until next month. ① can be repeated; in Month 12, ⑤ and ⑥ can follow in the same month.
  • 最快 7 月完工。因為要跨月，2 月又封山：1 月①、3 月②、4 月③、5 月④、6 月⑤、7 月⑥。
  • Month 7 at the earliest, because of the waiting and the closed forest in Month 2: ① in 1, ② in 3, ③ in 4, ④ in 5, ⑤ in 6, ⑥ in 7.

填縫是什麼？What is caulking?接合船板時，用 Avaka 纖維以 S 型纏繞木釘，壓實蘭嶼花椒（varok）根部的棉絮纖維，船板之間就不會漏水。用 varok 根部的棉絮替大船填縫，族語稱 mamaruk su cinedkeran nu varok，也說 tumbek su varok（Arnaud，2001）。When the planks are joined, Avaka fibre is wound round the wooden pegs in an S and presses down the cotton-like root fibre of the Lanyu prickly ash (varok), sealing the seams. Caulking a large boat with varok root fibre is called mamaruk su cinedkeran nu varok, or tumbek su varok (Arnaud, 2001).

在商店完工的兩條路Finishing at the Store

做完山林的三道工序、帶 2 份材料到商店，而且距離上一道工序已經跨月，就可以花 3 AP 直接完工。按下之前，畫面會先讓你聽聽耆老和中生代怎麼說。9 月和 10 月不能在商店完工。

With the three forest steps done, 2 materials in hand and a month passed since your last step, you can finish at the Store for 3 AP. Before you confirm, the screen lets you hear what the Elder and the Middle Gen. think. The Store cannot finish boats in Months 9 and 10.

全組一起的事Things the group shares

  • 每月小組任務：每個月抽一個全組一起完成的任務，例如「本月有人送材料給耆老」。達成後全組每人 +1 分。
  • Monthly group mission: each month draws one task for the whole group, such as “someone gives the Elder material”. Done: +1 for everyone.
  • 森林：0 到 5，開局 3。這個月有人上山砍伐、補種或巡山就 +1，整個月沒人去就 −1（2 月不算）。降到 1 以下，砍伐要多花 1 AP；年底在 4 以上，全組每人 +2 分。
  • The forest: 0 to 5, starting at 3. +1 in any month someone chops, replants or walks the hill; −1 if nobody goes (Month 2 aside). At 1 or below, chopping costs 1 AP more; at 4 or above at year end, everyone +2.
  • 部落的船：自己的船完工後，可以到灘頭每次花 1 AP 為全組共同的船出力，目標是人數 × 3 格。年底造完全組每人 +3 分，過半 +1 分；出力的人不另外加分。
  • The community boat: once your own boat is done, put 1 AP at a time into the group’s shared boat at the Shore; the goal is 3 sections per player. Complete at year end: everyone +3; half built: +1. Working on it earns nothing extra for yourself.

會跳出來的小視窗Pop-ups along the way

它們都不會讓你失去已經有的東西：答錯、不選、沒按準，只是少拿一點好處。

None of them can take away what you have: a wrong answer, a skipped choice or a missed tap only means a smaller bonus.

  • 請益小問答：每次請益後一題三選一，題目都在遊戲的知識庫裡。答對 +1 KP。
  • The asking quiz: one multiple-choice question after each ask, drawn from the game’s knowledge base. Right: +1 KP.
  • 事件卡：2、3、6、8、10、11 月開始時，每人一張二選一，例如 3 月「族人找你一起出海」：去的話 −2 AP、+1 分，留下來沒有變化。
  • Event cards: at the start of Months 2, 3, 6, 8, 10 and 11, a two-way choice for each player, such as Month 3’s “your relatives ask you out fishing”: go for −2 AP and +1, or stay with no change.
  • 工序小遊戲：做完一道工序後，指針走到綠色區域時按下，退回 1 AP。可以跳過。
  • The timing game: after a step, tap when the pointer is in the green to win back 1 AP. You can skip it.

船完工之後After your boat is done

一年還沒結束。你還可以移動、砍伐備料、送材料、補種、巡山、搭把手、科學轉譯（只得材料）、不限次數幫忙長者，或去灘頭為部落的船出力。不能再做的是請益、其他工序和在商店完工。

The year goes on. You can still walk, chop for others, give material, replant, walk the hill, lend a hand, translate (for material only), help the Elder as often as you like, or work on the community boat. What you can no longer do is ask, take other steps or finish at the Store.

結局Endings

分數不是在評斷哪條路對，而是讓你回頭看自己做了什麼。

The score does not judge which path was right; it shows you what you did.

第 12 個月結束後，加總完工的分數和這一年得到的分數，每個人得到自己的結局。遊戲中隨時可以在「得分」看到分數從哪裡來。

When Month 12 ends, each player’s finishing points and the points earned through the year are added up, and each reaches their own ending. In the game, “Score” shows where your points came from at any time.

  1. 傳承之靈Spirit of Heritage18 分以上，走傳統路徑18+, traditional path

    這艘船是活的：可以拆、可以修，帶著耆老說過的話繼續航行。

    The boat is alive: it can be opened and mended, and sails on carrying what the elders said.

    「這艘船是活的。可以拆，可以修，可以一塊一塊換掉，再一次出海。」“This boat is alive. It can be opened, mended, replaced plank by plank and sent out again.”
  2. 協商之光Light of Negotiation11 分以上11+

    在傳統與現實之間找到縫隙，船帶著工業的氣味，也帶著老人家的智慧。

    You found room between tradition and daily life; the boat smells of industry and carries the elders’ wisdom.

    「蘭嶼從來就不是一座博物館，人們在這裡過的是真實的生活，傳統也一直在真實的生活裡摩擦、磨損、又重新長出新的樣子。」“Lanyu was never a museum; people live real lives here, and tradition has always rubbed, worn and grown again within them.”
  3. 效率之重Weight of Efficiency6 到 10 分6 to 10

    The boat is built, but cannot be mended, and your hands forgot the pull of the fibre.

    「這艘船能用，但卻沒辦法教會你任何事。它只是一件時代下的商品。」“The boat works, but it cannot teach you anything. It is a product of its time.”
  4. 失落之魂Lost Soul5 分以下5 or less

    船下水了，卻繞過了所有真正重要的事。

    The boat is in the water, but everything that mattered was walked around.

    「你繞過了所有看起來麻煩的環節，也繞過了所有真正重要的事。」“You walked round everything that looked troublesome, and round everything that mattered.”

    纖維會腐爛，沒說出口的技術也會跟著保有記憶的人老去。

    The fibre will rot, and the knowledge never spoken will age with those who hold it.

    「那些纖維會腐爛。那些還沒說出口的技術知識，也會隨著保有記憶的人慢慢老去，而跟著消失。」“The fibre will rot. The knowledge never spoken will age with those who remember it, and be gone.”

請益與指導的加分，每人每月最多算 2 次，超過照樣得到 KP。徽章與年終稱號（最會傳承、最懂回饋、長者的幫手、山林守護者）不加分。

Points for asking and guiding count at most twice per player per month; beyond that you still gain KP. Badges and year-end titles (Best at Passing On, Most Giving, The Elder’s Helper, Guardian of the Forest) carry no points.
